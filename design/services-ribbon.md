# Services ribbon: the flow continues

Created with the built-in image generation tool as a new artwork. The hero was a material and palette reference only. Its separate strands become one; the services illustration carries that unified ribbon calmly from the left side of the page to the right.

## Files

- Master: [yumani-services-ribbon-master.png](./source/yumani-services-ribbon-master.png), 2172 × 724, RGBA with genuine transparency.
- Responsive assets: `public/images/yumani-services-ribbon-{720,1440,2160}.{avif,webp}`.
- The native transparent master is preserved; delivery formats only resize and encode it.

## Placement

A static 3:1 stage spans the full section width with a small overhang, so the ribbon enters and leaves through the page edges. No rotation or stretching is applied. Desktop opacity is 34%, with a lighter mask behind the accordion. Mobile opacity is 22%. The existing section layout determines height, while the separate background layer clips the artwork without clipping links or focus outlines. The image is decorative, lazy-loaded and hidden from assistive technology.

The asset was inspected independently against the pale page background. This pass did not include browser visual testing.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: one transparent raster website background decoration for the services section immediately below a hero.

Primary request: Generate exactly ONE brand-new isolated ribbon artwork, a calm continuation of the existing hero ribbon. The hero's four separate strands have already gathered into one unified ribbon; this artwork is the next chapter: that ONE unified ribbon now flowing calmly from the left side of the page to the right side.

Reference role: The previously inspected yumani-flow-master.png is ONLY a material and color-palette reference. Use its photorealistic translucent ice-blue satin-glass surface, fine longitudinal grain, soft periwinkle highlights and occasional restrained cobalt underside. Do not copy its geometry, four strands, branching, large arch, looping silhouette, or dramatic folds.

Composition/framing: A panoramic 3:1 canvas, ideally 3072 × 1024 pixels (1536 × 512 also suitable). One long, slim, uninterrupted ribbon enters through the left canvas edge at about 60% of the image height, gently rises and rolls in one shallow flowing S-curve, and continues all the way through and beyond the right canvas edge at about 40–55% of the image height. Its overall direction is clearly horizontal, spanning side to side. The ribbon's complete vertical envelope stays within the middle 45% of the canvas. Ample empty transparent space above and below. No exposed ribbon endpoints inside the canvas. A single subtle twist reveals the broad satin face and a thin edge; fewer folds than the reference.

Style/medium: Premium photorealistic three-dimensional satin-glass ribbon with delicate longitudinal fibers, translucent ice-blue material, satin pearlescence, soft periwinkle highlights, and very restrained cobalt on the underside. The material should be clearly visible and usable at full opacity; CSS will control subtlety later.

Lighting/mood: Hopeful morning studio light, gentle dimensional highlights contained on the material. Calm, elegant, graceful, visually quiet enough to sit beneath informational text.

Scene/backdrop: GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background with an actual alpha channel. Isolated ribbon only. No backdrop, no floor, no ground shadow, no vignette or atmospheric halo outside the ribbon.

Constraints: Exactly one continuous unified strip. No forks, no separate strands, no split tips, no loops, no knots, no closed infinity shape, no branches, no sculpture-like standing form, no dramatic flourish. No text, no logo, no watermark, no UI. New geometry; reference used only for material and palette.
