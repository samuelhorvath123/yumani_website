# About Us artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Source: `design/source/yumani-about-master.png`. Responsive assets in `public/images/yumani-about-{640,960,1536}.{avif,webp,jpg}`: AVIF (sharp, quality 50) and WebP (quality 72) are served first; the JPEGs remain only as the fallback for browsers without either.

Prompt: Create a standalone landscape brand artwork for Yumani Automation, a small custom software and automation team with a hopeful, human view of technology. Two broad, delicate translucent sheets of blue silk or handmade tracing paper fold toward each other into one open, sheltering arc. Their touching edges catch a fine thread of daylight. Tactile, naturally imperfect material photography, subtle fibers and luminous blue-white edges. 3:2 composition with the forms mainly in the right two thirds and calm negative space at left. Soft cobalt environment, periwinkle and pale cyan illumination, diffuse morning light. No people or faces, typography, logo, stars, neon, technological grid or extra objects. Not an infinity symbol, knot, chrome or plastic.

The team stage deliberately uses labelled preview slots until actual names, roles and portraits are supplied; each slot holds a different crop of this artwork (`yumani-about-640.webp`) so the layout can be judged as it will read. Add confirmed members to `lib/team.ts`; the preview slots and labels disappear automatically. Portraits should be local assets, 4:5 (840 by 1050), with optional `portraitPosition` for individual crops. Portraits sit on a deep blue room and the ones further from the middle are drawn into that blue, so shoot them in the same light and against the same kind of background and they will read as one set.
