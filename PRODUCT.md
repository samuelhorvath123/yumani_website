# Yumani Automation

## Register
brand

## Purpose and audience
Yumani Automation s.r.o. builds web applications and custom software, integrates existing systems, and adds practical AI to them, for businesses and institutions. The landing page should help prospective clients understand the offer and begin an email conversation with the team at info@yumaniautomation.com.

## Brand
Hopeful, attentive, determined. A small, hands-on team with ambition and the willingness to tackle hard problems. Professional but approachable. Quality is an explicit commitment; no invented statistics, customer logos, testimonials, certifications or business results.

## Principles
- Technology should return time and attention to people.
- Describe practical work and outcomes in plain English.
- Treat every organisation as having specific needs.
- Pair a confident future-facing presence with warm human language.
- Make the path to a conversation straightforward.

## Visual reference
The supplied DeepSeek screenshot informs the light blue palette, breathing room and calm confidence. Its logo, content and product UI are not part of Yumani's identity. Avoid dystopian robotics, neon, dark futuristic dashboards, generic stock teams and exaggerated claims.

## Scope
A responsive English landing page, with service disclosures, approach, values and direct email contact. A contact survey at /contact that emails each answer to the team, with nothing stored on the site, and no analytics. Privacy, cookie and terms pages and an opt-in cookie banner cover what the site actually does, which today is one first-party cookie that remembers the visitor's choice. Keyboard access and reduced-motion preferences are supported. Public release and a custom domain are separate from the private review deployment.
